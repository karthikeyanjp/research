## Executive Summary

Our research plan to implement a structured **Cursor AI workflow** positions the organization to achieve measurable productivity gains while maintaining strong governance and code quality.

## 1. Traditional SDLC vs. AI-Enhanced SDLC with Cursor

### Traditional SDLC (Manual & Bottlenecked)
- **Requirements**: Long meetings and manual documentation
- **Design**: Static diagrams and siloed discussions
- **Development**: Heavy manual coding + repetitive boilerplate
- **Testing**: Hand-written or minimal tests
- **Review & Deploy**: PR bottlenecks and slow feedback loops
- **Cycle Time**: Weeks to months per feature

### AI-Powered SDLC with Cursor (Agentic & Accelerated)
- **Requirements**: AI-generated user stories, acceptance criteria, and Jira tickets
- **Design**: Architecture suggestions, diagrams, and cross-file awareness
- **Development**: Agentic coding, multi-file edits, and `.cursorrules` enforcement
- **Testing**: Auto-generated unit/integration tests with human validation
- **Review & Deploy**: AI-assisted PR reviews + seamless CI/CD integration
- **Cycle Time**: Compressed from weeks to days (with governance)

**Visual Recommendation**: Side-by-side flowchart with red (traditional) → green (AI) color shift and speed icons.

## 2. Core AI Efficiency Practices

- **Planner-First Mindset**: Humans create high-level plans and architecture; Cursor Agents execute well-scoped tasks
- **Context Engineering**: Use `@files`/`@folders` + custom `.cursorrules` for coding standards, security, and architecture guardrails
- **Agentic Workflows**: Separate chats per task to preserve context, voice mode for rapid iteration, Composer for complex multi-file changes
- **Human-in-the-Loop Governance**: Mandatory human review on critical flows (auth, payments, security, compliance) + automated testing
- **Toil Reduction**: Automate boilerplate, refactoring, documentation, and debugging — freeing engineers for innovation and complex problem-solving

**Visual Recommendation**: Radial icons around a central Cursor logo.

### Sample ROI Calculation (Conservative – 20-Developer Pilot)
**Assumptions**:
- Average fully-loaded developer cost: $175/hour
- Savings: 4 hours/week per developer (conservative with governance)
- 48 working weeks/year
- Cursor Teams pricing: ~$40/user/month

**Annual Value Created**:  
20 devs × 4 hrs/week × 48 weeks × $175/hr = **~$672,000**

**Annual Tool Cost**: ~$9,600 (plus modest token usage)

**Net Benefit**: ~$662,400  
**ROI on tool cost**: **~69x** (extremely high)

Even with more conservative assumptions (2 hours/week savings + token costs), ROI remains in the **5–10x+** range in Year 1, improving further as processes mature.

**Broader Business Value**:
- Faster time-to-market and competitive advantage

## 5. Risks & Mitigations (Transparency for CIO)

- **Over-reliance on AI**: Addressed by planner-first workflows and mandatory human oversight on critical paths
- **Quality or security concerns**: Enforced via `.cursorrules`, automated testing, and review protocols
- **Cost variability**: Managed through scoped usage and prompt best practices
- **Learning curve**: Mitigated by phased pilot and hands-on training
